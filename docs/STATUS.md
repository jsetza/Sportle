- **Split screen (owner request):** with two players on one screen and a player camera (third/first person), the display splits into left (player 1) and right (player 2) halves, each with its own eased camera, first-person paddle and name tags; broadcast stays a single shared view (V switches). - **Faster volleys and overheads (owner request):** attacking volleys (hit before the bounce) and smashes fly 1.75× faster (`VOLLEY_SPEEDUP`); dink and lob volleys keep their touch; a net-clearance check keeps the flatter ball over the net. Test added (54 passing); bot matches still finish with no side bias.
- **Swing strength sets ball speed (owner request):** forehand/backhand drives travel at a speed proportional to the swing percentage (`GROUND_MAX_SPEED` 12.5 m/s at 100%, scaled by strength^0.8); soft swings are slower, and dinks slow with lower strength too. Test added.
- **Ball slows in flight (owner request):** the ball is fastest off the paddle and loses 15% of its horizontal speed every 0.2 s (`BALL_DRAG`). Launches compensate so shots still land where aimed, capped at 3× the shot's average speed (`MAX_LAUNCH_FACTOR`); bot/auto-positioning predictions use the same slowdown. 56 tests passing; browser smoke passes; bot sims still finish both ways (solo hard 8–4, co-op hard 6–6). Feel on real phones is untested.
- **Consistent hitbox under lag (owner report: "hitbox still isn't consistent"):** players swing at what their screen shows, which runs behind the server (stream + phone network delay). Before, a swing arriving more than ~75 ms after the best moment missed outright, so the same swing hit or missed depending on that moment's lag. Now the server remembers each human's hittable moments for `LATE_GRACE` (0.22 s) and a late swing is played at the best of them (the ball is put back there), as long as nobody touched the ball and it hasn't bounced again since. A second bounce / ball leaving the area waits for that chance before the point is called. Early swings wait `EARLY_WINDOW` (0.25 s, was 0.12) for the ball; human reach 1.3 m (`HUMAN_REACH`, bots unchanged at 1.15 m). Sim (/tmp/sim/lag.ts, 40 practice feeds per timing): old 40/40 hits at 0–50 ms late, 0/40 at ≥100 ms; new 40/40 from 300 ms early to 200 ms late, average quality ≥0.94. Test added (57 passing); bot balance unchanged. Real-phone lag not yet measured.
